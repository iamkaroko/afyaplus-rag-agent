# AfyaPlus Clinical Routing Guidelines

## Emergency Symptoms

Patients reporting any of the following symptoms should be routed to emergency care immediately:

- Severe difficulty breathing
- Loss of consciousness
- Heavy uncontrolled bleeding
- Severe chest pain
- Signs of stroke such as facial weakness, arm weakness, or difficulty speaking
- Seizures lasting more than five minutes

## Urgent Clinical Review

Patients should receive urgent clinical review when they report:

- Persistent high fever
- Moderate breathing difficulty
- Severe headache with neurological symptoms
- Persistent vomiting with signs of dehydration
- Rapidly worsening pain

## Routine Care

Stable patients with mild symptoms that are not worsening may be routed to routine outpatient care.

Examples include:

- Mild cold symptoms
- Stable chronic medication review
- Routine follow-up appointments
- Non-urgent insurance benefit questions
