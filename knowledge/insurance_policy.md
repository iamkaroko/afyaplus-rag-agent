# AfyaPlus Insurance Policy

## MRI Coverage

MRI scans are covered when requested by a registered physician.

Outpatient MRI scans require pre-authorisation before the procedure is performed.

Emergency diagnostic imaging does not require prior authorisation when the attending clinician determines that delaying imaging would create a significant risk to the patient.

The standard outpatient MRI co-payment is KES 2,000.

## CT Scan Coverage

CT scans are covered when clinically justified by a registered physician.

Routine outpatient CT scans require pre-authorisation.

Emergency CT scans may proceed without prior authorisation when immediate imaging is medically necessary.

## Specialist Consultations

Specialist consultations are covered when the patient has a valid referral from a registered primary care clinician.

Emergency specialist consultations do not require a referral.

## Laboratory Tests

Routine laboratory tests are covered when ordered by a registered clinician.

Specialised laboratory tests may require pre-authorisation depending on the patient's benefit plan.
